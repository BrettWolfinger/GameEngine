Detailed Description

Engine collision facade — register, update, and remove colliders.

Colliders are identified by an opaque ColliderHandle. Store the handle as a member on any object that needs to update or remove its collider. Use NULL_COLLIDER as a sentinel for "no collider registered."

Layer/mask filtering: a collision is reported when (a.layer & b.mask) || (b.layer & a.mask). Assign layers as powers of two and combine them with bitwise OR to build masks.
